## Overview

- Explore, clean, prepare for analysis and [ML](Machine%20Learning.md)
- Can detect schemas—data types, joins, anomalies
- Prediction of next transformation
- Transformation functions, e.g. join, union, aggregate, merge, pivot
- Inputs: CSV, JSON, relational data
	- From: [Cloud Storage](Cloud%20Storage.md), [BigQuery](BigQuery.md), direct upload
- Outputs:
	- To: [BigQuery](BigQuery.md), [Dataflow](Dataflow.md), file
